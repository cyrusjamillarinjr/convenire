STAGE 3: 5-STEP RJ-45 MODULAR CABLE CRIMPING

Simulate authentic NCII standard network cable fabrication following the official T568B telecommunications standard.

STEP 01

STRIP JACKET

Rotate cable stripper around outer PVC sheath. Carefully slide off jacket without nicking the 4 twisted copper pairs.

STEP 02

UNPAIR & ALIGN

Untwist wire pairs and arrange left-to-right according to T568B:

1. W-Org 2. Orange 3. W-Grn 4. Blue 5. W-Blu 6. Green 7. W-Brn 8. Brown
STEP 03

FLUSH TRIM

Flatten wires into a parallel ribbon and cut flush at a 90° angle, leaving exactly 1/2 inch (12-14mm) exposed length.

STEP 04

PLUG INSERTION

Slide aligned wires into transparent RJ-45 8P8C modular plug with golden pins facing up until copper reaches the tip.

STEP 05

RATCHET CRIMP

Insert plug into 8P die and squeeze ratchet handles firmly until tool completes full cycle to drive gold pins into copper conductors.
